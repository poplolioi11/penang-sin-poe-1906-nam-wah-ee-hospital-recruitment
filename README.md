# 檳城南華醫院招考與發榜（《檳城新報》，1906）（檳城，1906年8月8日、9月20日，《檳城新報》兩篇本埠新聞。新加坡國立大學圖書館。） / Bincheng Nanhua yiyuan zhao kao yu fa bang [The Nam Wah Ee Hospital Recruitment Examination and Results]: Transcription Dataset

《檳城新報》1906年8月8日〈南華醫院考試醫生〉和9月20日〈醫師中選〉記錄檳城南華醫院同一次醫師招考。首篇記有47人應試、抽選試題並稱將把試卷封送香港東華醫院；後篇則稱試卷送到廣仁善堂評閱，列出兩名錄用者。兩篇可供比對華人慈善醫院的醫師聘任、考試安排與跨埠評閱。

Two local reports in the Penang Sin Poe, dated 8 August and 20 September 1906, document the same recruitment examination at Nam Wah Ee Hospital in Penang. The first records 47 candidates and reports a plan to send sealed papers to Tung Wah Hospital in Hong Kong. The second names Guangren Shantang as the reported recipient and identifies two successful applicants. Read together, they provide evidence of recruitment and reported external assessment at a Chinese charitable hospital.

## 方法與覆核 / Methods and review

由 AI 依固定館方影像逐欄讀字，保留原字、版面與疑讀。逐報保留原標題、換欄位置、字形及圈點。原報兩篇時序、刊期及各自說法一同標記。人類全文雙校、專家審定、同儕審查及準確率量測尚未進行。實際模型識別與使用量未知。此資料公開供比對與引用，處理程式及影像另存。

An AI assistant transcribed the fixed source images column by column, preserving source forms, layout and uncertain readings. The original titles, column transitions, original figures and readings, issue dates and the statements attributed to each newspaper item are preserved.
